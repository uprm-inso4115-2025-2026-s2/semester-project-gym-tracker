= Risk Analysis for Requirements Prioritization
:toc:
:toclevels: 2

== Overview

This document presents workflow diagrams for identifying, analyzing, and mitigating risks associated with system requirements. The objective is to improve requirement quality and reduce potential development issues.

---

== Workflow Diagram

----
+------------------------------+
|      Review Requirements     |
|------------------------------|
|    Features, Stakeholders    |
+-------------+----------------+
              |
              v
+------------------------------+
|       Identify Risks         |
|------------------------------|
|  Ambiguity, Conflicts, Scope |
+-------------+----------------+
              |
              v
+------------------------------+
|        Classify Risks        |
|------------------------------|
|      Likelihood + Impact     |
+-------------+----------------+
              |
              v
+------------------------------+
|     Evaluate Risk Level      |
|------------------------------|
|     Determine Priority       |
+-------------+----------------+
              |
              v
+------------------------------+
|    Mitigation Strategies     |
|------------------------------|
|  Clarify, Validate, Review   |
+-------------+----------------+
              |
              v
+------------------------------+
|      Risk Table Output       |
+------------------------------+
----

=== Explanation

- *Review Requirements*: Analyze all defined system features and requirements.
- *Identify Risks*: Detect issues such as ambiguity or missing requirements.
- *Classify Risks*: Assign likelihood and impact levels.
- *Evaluate Risk*: Determine which risks require immediate attention.
- *Mitigation Strategies*: Define actions to reduce or eliminate risks.
- *Output*: Produce a structured risk analysis table.

---

== Risk Decision Flow

----
        +----------------------+
        |   Identified Risk    |
        +----------+-----------+
                   |
        +----------+-----------+
        |                      |
        v                      v
   High Impact?           Low Impact?
        |                      |
       YES                    YES
        |                      |
        v                      v
 High Likelihood?         Monitor Only
        |
   +----+-----------------+
   |                      |
  YES                     NO
   |                      |
   v                      v
Immediate Action     Mitigation Plan

----

=== Explanation

- High impact + high likelihood → *Immediate action required*
- High impact + low likelihood → *Mitigation planning*
- Low impact risks → *Monitoring*

This ensures efficient allocation of effort.
