=== Domain Constraints and Invariants for Streaks
:author: Ariana Rodriguez

The streak system depends on a collection of domain constraints and
invariants that preserve consistency within the User Activity Log and streak
evaluation process. Active Streaks do not exist independently. Instead, they
emerge from the ordered sequence of Workout Completions, Consecutive
Workouts, and Missed Days recorded over time. Because streak continuity
depends on these relationships remaining valid, identifying the rules
governing streak behavior is necessary to preserve reliability within the
domain.

Workout qualification forms the foundation of streak evaluation. A Workout
Session only contributes to streak progression when it satisfies the criteria
required for Workout Completion. Workout Sessions that do not satisfy these
requirements cannot contribute to an Active Streak. This rule ensures that
streak progression is based on validated activity rather than the existence 
of a recorded session.

Workout Completion and Missed Days establish a fundamental invariant within
the domain. For streak evaluation, a calendar day must have a single
participation outcome. A day may contribute to streak progression through a
valid Workout Completion, or it may be considered a Missed Day. A day cannot
simultaneously represent both outcomes. This invariant preserves consistency by
ensuring that every day contributes a single state to streak evaluation.

Active Streaks emerge from Consecutive Workouts performed on sequential
eligible days. Individual Workout Completions do not create meaningful streak
behavior on their own. Instead, streaks develop through repeated workout
participation across uninterrupted calendar-day sequences. This rule ensures
that Active Streak values accurately represent consistency over time rather
than isolated workout activity.

As workout activity continues across consecutive days, each additional
Consecutive Workout may extend an existing Active Streak. Streak progression
therefore depends on maintaining uninterrupted workout participation across
time. The absence of continuity prevents further streak growth.

Missed Days directly affect this progression. A Missed Day represents a day
in which the required activity criteria were not satisfied. Because an Active
Streak represents uninterrupted participation, a Missed Day interrupts the
sequence of Consecutive Workouts from which the Active Streak is derived. Once
continuity is interrupted, streak progression can no longer continue as part
of the same sequence.

The relationship between Missed Days and Active Streaks must also remain
consistent. A Missed Day can never belong to an Active Streak. If a Missed Day
occurs between two periods of workout participation, the workout activity
before and after the interruption belongs to different streak sequence. This
preserves the meaning of continuity throughout the domain.

Streak evaluation also depends on the temporal ordering of information within
the User Activity Log. Workout Completions, Consecutive Workouts, and Missed
Days must always be interpreted in chronological order. Evaluating activity
records out of sequence may produce continuity results that do not accurately
reflect actual workout behavior.

Workout quantity introduces an additional invariant. Multiple Workout
Sessions may occur during a single calendar day. However, regardless of the
number of sessions performed, the day contributes only one valid Workout
Completion toward Active Streak evaluation. This prevents artificial inflation
of streak progression and preserves consistent interpretation of workout
participation.
