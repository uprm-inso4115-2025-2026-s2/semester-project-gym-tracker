==== [LTT]: Streaks Domain Glossary
:author: Jan Davey Morales Vazquez

== Overview

This glossary standardizes terminology used within the Streaks module of the gym tracker application. It is intended to ensure consistent language across development, testing, analytics, and product documentation.

All terms are defined in the context of user workout consistency tracking and related behavioral systems.

== Glossary

=== Active Streak
A continuous sequence of days in which a user has met the defined workout criteria without interruption.

=== Achievement System
The subsystem responsible for tracking, evaluating, and awarding user accomplishments such as badges, milestones, and rewards.

=== Badge Unlock
The event in which a user earns and receives a badge after fulfilling predefined conditions within the streak or challenge systems.

=== Challenge Participation
The state in which a user is actively enrolled in a structured goal or task with defined rules and duration, often influencing streak progression or rewards.

=== Consistency Score
A computed metric representing how consistently a user completes workouts over a defined time window, factoring in streaks, missed days, and activity frequency.

=== Consecutive Workout
A workout session that occurs on sequential eligible days contributing directly to an active streak.

=== Daily Goal
A user-specific or system-defined target that must be completed within a day to qualify as a valid workout contribution toward streaks.

=== Missed Day
A day in which the user did not meet the required activity criteria, potentially impacting or breaking an active streak depending on recovery rules.

=== Milestone Reward
A predefined reward granted when a user reaches specific streak lengths or consistency thresholds.

=== Notification Reminder
A system-generated prompt intended to encourage users to complete their daily workout goal and maintain their streak.

=== Progress Tracking
The continuous monitoring and recording of user workout activity and streak progression over time.

=== Recovery Window
A limited time period during which a user may restore or preserve an active streak after a missed or incomplete workout day, depending on system rules.

=== Rest Day
A day intentionally or system-recognized as non-active, which may or may not affect streak continuity depending on configuration.

=== Streak Freeze
A mechanism that allows a user to preserve their active streak despite not meeting daily activity requirements for a limited number of occurrences.

=== Streak Reset
The event in which a user's active streak is returned to zero due to failure to meet continuity rules beyond allowable exceptions (e.g., freezes or recovery windows).

=== Weekly Target
A higher-level goal representing the number of workouts or active days a user should complete within a week.

=== Workout Completion
A validated workout session that satisfies system criteria to count toward streak progression and other metrics.

=== Workout Session
A recorded instance of user physical activity within the system, which may or may not qualify as a completed workout depending on rules.

=== User Activity Log
A structured record of all user interactions and workout-related events used for analytics, streak calculation, and historical tracking.

=== Longest Streak
The maximum number of consecutive active streak days achieved by a user over their lifetime in the system.
