==== 2.2.9 AHP Prioritization of Criteria and Requirements

[.added]#This subsection records the prioritization analysis requested in issue 347. Because the repository and issue history do not preserve recoverable per-member pairwise ballots, the analysis is documented as one consolidated team synthesis grounded in the current merged goals, stakeholder concerns, risks, and requirements. The calculation artifact committed in `docs/support/ahp_prioritization.py` reproduces the matrices, priority vectors, and consistency ratios summarized below.#

===== AHP Method Used

[.added]#The prioritization is carried out at two levels. First, the evaluation criteria are compared pairwise. Second, five candidate requirements are compared pairwise under each criterion. The final requirement priority is computed by combining the local requirement priorities with the criteria weights. This keeps the section aligned with the AHP method instead of reducing it to a direct scoring table.#

[.added]#The five candidate requirements were selected because they concentrate the main user-facing and reliability concerns of the current milestone: Persistent Storage and Retrieval, Streak Calculation, Low-Effort Logging, Goal Evaluation, and Editing and Correction of Workout Data.#

===== Limitation of the Current Evidence

[.added]#This subsection still does not claim full satisfaction of the original issue wording. The current repository does not preserve each team member's individual pairwise matrices, and it does not preserve prior R `ahp` package output. What is now present is a documented, reproducible, branch-local AHP synthesis with explicit matrices, consistency checks, and final priorities. That is materially stronger than the previous absence of AHP evidence, but it is not a substitute for archived member-by-member input.#
