==== 2.1.2 Terminology

===== Domain Concepts

#*Activity* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A physical action performed for fitness purposes, such as running,
lifting weights, or performing cardio.

#*Exercise* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A structured unit of activity performed within a workout, typically
characterized by repetitions, duration, or intensity.

#*Workout* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A bounded period of physical training during which one or more
Exercises or Activities are performed.

#*Workout Session* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A specific occurrence of a Workout performed by a GymGoer at a
particular time and on a particular Calendar Day.

#*Workout History* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
An ordered collection of Workout Sessions associated with a GymGoer
across time.

#*GymGoer* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
An individual who performs Workout Sessions across time and may be
associated with one or more Goals related to fitness activity.

#*Calendar Day* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A single day used to locate Workout Sessions in time and to determine
whether activity occurred on that day.

#*Attendance Day* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A Calendar Day on which at least one Workout Session has occurred.

#*Missed Day* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A Calendar Day on which no Workout Session has occurred despite an
intended or expected workout pattern.

#*Time Frame* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A bounded temporal interval within which activity or a Goal is
evaluated, such as a day or a week.

#*Week* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A seven-day Time Frame composed of named Weekday values and used for
weekly aggregation, Goal evaluation, and summary generation.

#*Goal* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A desired level of activity or performance defined within a Time Frame,
such as completing a certain Goal Target Count of Attendance Days in a
Week.

#*Goal Target Count* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A positive integer count of Attendance Days required for a Goal within
its Time Frame.

[.underline]#*Duration* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
[.underline]#A positive quantity expressing how long a Workout Session lasted, measured in minutes. Duration is an attribute of a Workout Session, not a standalone entity — it is the domain-level name for the elapsed time of a session.#

#*Weekday* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
One of the seven named days of the week used when a requirement needs a
day-of-week value separate from a full Calendar Day.

#*Workout Notes* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
Short written annotations describing what occurred during a Workout
Session or how it felt.

#*Reminder* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A prompt or cue intended to encourage activity when no recent Workout
Session has occurred. It is a domain entity because it can exist
independently of software, for example as an alarm, calendar note,
sticky note, or verbal reminder.

#*Weekly Summary* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
An overview of Workout Sessions, Attendance Days, Missed Days, and Goal
evaluation within a Week.

#*Session Comparison* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A representation of observable differences between two Workout
Sessions, such as duration, activity type, or exercise composition.

#*Consistency Measure* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A representation of how regularly Workout Sessions occur across a Time
Frame.

#*Goal Evaluation* (Domain Concept, Category: Action)#
The act of determining whether a Goal has been achieved within its Time
Frame.

#*Goal Result* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
The evaluated outcome of a Goal Evaluation, including whether the Goal
was achieved and how many target days remain.

#*Workout Session has just been performed* (Domain Concept, Category: Event)#
An event in which a Workout Session has occurred at a specific time on
a specific Calendar Day.

#*Workout Session has just been skipped* (Domain Concept, Category: Event)#
An event in which an expected opportunity for workout activity has
passed without a Workout Session occurring.

#*Goal period has just ended* (Domain Concept, Category: Event)#
An event in which the Time Frame associated with a Goal has completed.

#*Streak has just been extended* (Domain Concept, Category: Event)#
An event in which a Workout Session has occurred on the Calendar Day
immediately following an Attendance Day, preserving consecutiveness.

#*Streak has just been broken* (Domain Concept, Category: Event)#
An event in which a Missed Day has occurred after one or more
Attendance Days, ending consecutiveness.

[.underline]#*recordWorkout(gymGoer: GymGoer, workout: Workout, date: CalendarDay, notes: WorkoutNotes) : GymGoer >< WorkoutSession* (Domain Concept, Category: Action)#
[.underline]#An action that creates a Workout Session for a GymGoer on a given Calendar Day and returns both the updated GymGoer (whose Workout History now contains the new session) and the newly created WorkoutSession. The GymGoer is included in the result because recording a workout modifies the GymGoer's Workout History — the WorkoutSession exists within that history and cannot be observed independently of it.#

#*compareSessions(a: WorkoutSession, b: WorkoutSession) : SessionComparison* (Domain Concept, Category: Action)#
An action that determines observable differences between two Workout
Sessions, such as duration, activity type, or exercise composition.

#*evaluateConsistency(history: WorkoutHistory, timeFrame: TimeFrame) : ConsistencyMeasure* (Domain Concept, Category: Action)#
An action that determines how regularly Workout Sessions occur across a
specified Time Frame.

#*computeStreak(history: WorkoutHistory) : Streak* (Domain Concept, Category: Action)#
An action that determines the current Streak from the ordered
distribution of Attendance Days and Missed Days in a Workout History.

#*retrieveWorkoutHistory(gymGoer: GymGoer) : WorkoutHistory* (Domain Concept, Category: Action)#
An action that determines the Workout History associated with a given
GymGoer.

#*evaluateGoal(goal: Goal, history: WorkoutHistory) : GoalResult* (Domain Concept, Category: Action)#
An action that determines whether a Goal has been achieved within its
Time Frame based on the relevant Workout History.

#*summarizeWeek(history: WorkoutHistory, goal: Goal, week: Week) : WeeklySummary* (Domain Concept, Category: Action)#
An action that produces an overview of activity, missed days, and Goal
evaluation for a completed Week.

#*Weekly Activity Pattern* (Domain Concept, Category: Behavior)#
A behavior composed of Workout Session has just been performed and
Workout Session has just been skipped events across the Calendar Days of
a Week, together with the resulting distribution of Attendance Days and
Missed Days.

#*Consistency* (Domain Concept, Category: Behavior) A behavior describing how regularly Workout Sessions occur across a Time Frame, based on the distribution of Attendance Days and Missed Days in the relevant Workout History and interpreted through evaluateConsistency(…).#

#*Streak* (Domain Concept, Category: Behavior)#
A behavior composed of consecutive Attendance Days produced by repeated
Workout Session has just been performed events with no intervening
Missed Day. This behavior is extended by Streak has just been extended
and terminated by Streak has just been broken.

#*Progress* (Domain Concept, Category: Behavior)#
A behavior composed of changes observed across Workout Sessions,
Consistency Measures, Session Comparisons, and Goal Evaluations over
successive Time Frames.

===== Requirement-Level Concepts

#*User* (Requirement Concept, Category: Entity)#
A person whose activities and goals are tracked by the system.

#*Account* (Requirement Concept, Category: Entity)#
A persistent identity that allows a User's workout history and goals to
be stored.

#*Notification* (Requirement Concept, Category: Entity)#
A message delivered by the system to inform a User about reminders,
streak status, or goal progress.

#*Weekly Summary Table* (Requirement Concept, Category: Entity)#
A structured representation of weekly activity and goal evaluation
produced for review.

===== Implementation-Level Terms

#*Preset Entry* (Implementation Term, Category: Entity)#
A predefined workout or activity configuration used to speed up
logging.

#*Quick Entry Workflow* (Implementation Term, Category: Behavior)#
A sequence of interface steps designed to minimize the effort required
to record a workout.

#*Motivational Message* (Implementation Term, Category: Entity)#
Text produced by the system to reinforce consistency or progress. It is
kept as an implementation term rather than a domain concept because the
domain-level phenomenon is motivation or encouragement, while a
motivational message is a specific system-produced textual artifact.

===== Categorization Notes

#*Reminder* is categorized as an entity because this section refers to
the prompt or cue itself, which can exist independently of software. If
the focus were on the act of reminding, it could instead be modeled as
an action or event.#

#*Notification* is categorized as an entity because this section refers
to the delivered message itself. If the focus were on delivery, it
could instead be modeled as an event.#

#*Weekly Summary* is categorized as an entity, while summarizeWeek(...)
is categorized as an action. This distinguishes the produced artifact
from the domain action that produces it.#

#*Goal Evaluation* names the action of assessing whether a Goal was
achieved. *Goal Result* names the evaluated outcome produced by that
action.#

[.underline]#*Duration*, *Weekday*, and *Goal Target Count* are design-side constrained value types in this notation. They describe the required shape of values in the domain model, not the current TypeScript field names in the implementation section. Duration replaces the earlier term Minutes throughout all signatures and definitions to use the domain-appropriate name for elapsed session time.#

#*Streak*, *Consistency*, *Progress*, and *Weekly Activity Pattern* are
categorized as behaviors because they describe temporal compositions of
events and actions rather than standalone objects.#

===== #Terminology Completeness Validation#

#A terminology walkthrough was used to test whether the domain story
could be told using domain terms only.#

#Scenario used for validation: A Goal requires three Attendance Days
within one Week. In that Week, a Workout Session has just been
performed on Monday and Tuesday. These Workout Sessions belong to the
same Workout History and produce two Attendance Days. On Wednesday, a
Workout Session is skipped, so a Missed Day occurs and the emerging
Streak is broken. On Thursday, another Workout Session has just been
performed. The Workout History for that Week now contains three Workout
Sessions distributed across one Week, with three Attendance Days and one
Missed Day. From this Workout History, the domain can compute a Streak,
evaluate Consistency, evaluate the Goal within its Time Frame, and
produce a Weekly Summary.#

#Gap discovered during walkthrough: the scenario required the terms
Workout History, Calendar Day, Time Frame, Week, Goal Evaluation,
Goal Result, Weekly Summary, and the distinction between events,
actions, and behaviors within the same concept space. These concepts
were already used implicitly by the domain model, but they were not
previously defined in a unified and consistent way.#

#Resolution: the terminology was restructured so that entities, events,
actions, and behaviors all appear together under Domain Concepts. In
this structure, Workout History contains Workout Sessions; Attendance
Days and Missed Days are derived from the distribution of Workout
Sessions across Calendar Days; a Week is a Time Frame; Goal evaluation
is performed over a Time Frame; and behaviors such as Streak,
Consistency, Progress, and Weekly Activity Pattern are defined as
compositions of domain events and actions rather than as isolated
descriptions.#
