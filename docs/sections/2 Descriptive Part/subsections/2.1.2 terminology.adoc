==== 2.1.2 Terminology

===== Domain Concepts

[.changed]#*Activity* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A physical action performed for fitness purposes, such as running,
lifting weights, cycling, rowing, or performing cardio.

[.changed]#*Exercise* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A structured Activity performed within a Workout. Examples include
bench press, squats, pull-ups, treadmill running, or jump rope. An
Exercise may be described by repetitions, sets, distance, Duration, or
intensity depending on the Activity.

[.changed]#*Workout* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A bounded period of physical training during which one or more
Exercises or Activities are performed.

[.changed]#*Workout Session* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A specific occurrence of a Workout performed by a GymGoer on a
particular Calendar Day. A Workout Session may include a workout type,
Duration, Exercises, Activities, and optional Workout Notes.

[.changed]#*Workout History* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
An ordered collection of Workout Sessions associated with one GymGoer
across time. A Workout Session belongs to the Workout History of the
GymGoer who performed it.

[.changed]#*GymGoer* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
An individual who performs Workout Sessions across time and may be
associated with one or more Goals related to fitness activity. This term
is preferred over "User" when describing the person in the fitness
domain rather than their interaction with the system.

[.changed]#*Calendar Day* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A single day used to locate Workout Sessions in time and to determine
whether activity occurred on that day.

[.changed]#*Attendance Day* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A Calendar Day on which at least one Workout Session has occurred.

[.changed]#*Missed Day* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A Calendar Day on which no Workout Session has occurred despite an
intended or expected workout pattern.

[.changed]#*Time Frame* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A bounded temporal interval within which activity or a Goal is
evaluated, such as a day or a week.

[.changed]#*Week* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A seven-day Time Frame composed of named Weekday values and used for
weekly aggregation, Goal Evaluation, and Weekly Summary generation.

[.changed]#*Goal* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A desired level of activity or performance defined within a Time Frame,
such as completing a certain Goal Target Count of Attendance Days in a
Week.

[.changed]#*Goal Target Count* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A positive integer count of Attendance Days or Workout Sessions required
for a Goal within its Time Frame, depending on how the Goal is defined.

[.changed]#*Duration* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
The amount of time spent in a Workout Session or Exercise. Duration is
the domain concept. Minutes may be used as the measurement unit for
Duration, but Minutes is not the main domain term.

[.changed]#*Weekday* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
One of the seven named days of the week used when a requirement needs a
day-of-week value separate from a full Calendar Day.

[.changed]#*Workout Notes* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
Short written annotations attached to a Workout Session. Notes may
describe what occurred during the session, what felt difficult, what was
changed, or what should be remembered later.

[.changed]#*Reminder* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A prompt or cue intended to encourage activity when no recent Workout
Session has occurred. It is a domain entity because it can exist
independently of software, for example as an alarm, calendar note,
sticky note, or verbal reminder.

[.changed]#*Weekly Summary* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
An overview of a GymGoer’s Workout Sessions, Attendance Days, Missed
Days, Goal Result, and relevant comparison information for one Week.

[.changed]#*Session Comparison* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A representation of observable differences between two Workout
Sessions, such as Duration, Activity type, Exercise composition, Calendar
Day, or Workout Notes.

[.changed]#*Consistency Measure* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
A representation of how regularly a GymGoer performs Workout Sessions
across a Time Frame.

[.changed]#*Goal Evaluation* (Domain Concept, Category: Action)#
The act of determining whether a Goal has been achieved within its Time
Frame.

[.changed]#*Goal Result* (Domain Concept, Category: Entity)#
The evaluated outcome of a Goal Evaluation, including whether the Goal
was achieved and what activity count was reached.

[.changed]#*Workout Session has just been performed* (Domain Concept, Category: Event)#
An event in which a GymGoer finishes a Workout Session on a specific
Calendar Day.

[.changed]#*Workout Session has just been recorded* (Domain Concept, Category: Event)#
An event in which a performed Workout Session is added to the GymGoer’s
Workout History.

[.changed]#*Workout Session has just been corrected* (Domain Concept, Category: Event)#
An event in which an existing Workout Session is changed because the
GymGoer recorded incorrect or incomplete information.

[.changed]#*Workout Session has just been removed* (Domain Concept, Category: Event)#
An event in which a Workout Session is removed from a GymGoer’s Workout
History.

[.changed]#*Workout day has just been missed* (Domain Concept, Category: Event)#
An event in which a Calendar Day passes without a Workout Session even
though the GymGoer intended or expected activity on that day.

[.changed]#*Goal period has just ended* (Domain Concept, Category: Event)#
An event in which the Time Frame associated with a Goal has completed
and the Goal can be evaluated.

[.changed]#*Streak has just been extended* (Domain Concept, Category: Event)#
An event in which a Workout Session occurs on the Calendar Day
immediately following an Attendance Day, preserving consecutiveness.

[.changed]#*Streak has just been broken* (Domain Concept, Category: Event)#
An event in which a Missed Day occurs after one or more consecutive
Attendance Days, ending consecutiveness.

[.changed]#*Weekly Summary has just been generated* (Domain Concept, Category: Event)#
An event in which the GymGoer’s activity for a Week has been summarized.

[.changed]#*recordWorkout(gymGoer: GymGoer, workout: Workout, date: CalendarDay, duration: Duration, notes: WorkoutNotes) : GymGoer* (Domain Concept, Category: Action)#
An action that records a Workout Session for a GymGoer. The result is
the updated GymGoer, whose Workout History now contains the new Workout
Session.

[.changed]#*retrieveWorkoutHistory(gymGoer: GymGoer) : WorkoutHistory* (Domain Concept, Category: Action)#
An action that returns the Workout History associated with a given
GymGoer.

[.changed]#*findWorkoutSessions(history: WorkoutHistory, timeFrame: TimeFrame) : List<WorkoutSession>* (Domain Concept, Category: Action)#
An action that returns the Workout Sessions in a Workout History that
occurred within a Time Frame.

[.changed]#*findLastWorkoutSession(history: WorkoutHistory) : WorkoutSession* (Domain Concept, Category: Action)#
An action that returns the most recent Workout Session in a Workout
History.

[.changed]#*findSessionsByExercise(history: WorkoutHistory, exercise: Exercise) : List<WorkoutSession>* (Domain Concept, Category: Action)#
An action that returns the Workout Sessions in which a specific Exercise
was performed.

[.changed]#*compareSessions(a: WorkoutSession, b: WorkoutSession) : SessionComparison* (Domain Concept, Category: Action)#
An action that determines observable differences between two Workout
Sessions, such as Duration, Activity type, Calendar Day, Workout Notes,
or Exercise composition.

[.changed]#*evaluateConsistency(history: WorkoutHistory, timeFrame: TimeFrame) : ConsistencyMeasure* (Domain Concept, Category: Action)#
An action that determines how regularly Workout Sessions occur across a
specified Time Frame.

[.changed]#*computeStreak(history: WorkoutHistory) : Streak* (Domain Concept, Category: Action)#
An action that determines the current Streak from the ordered
distribution of Attendance Days and Missed Days in a Workout History.

[.changed]#*evaluateGoal(goal: Goal, history: WorkoutHistory) : GoalResult* (Domain Concept, Category: Action)#
An action that determines whether a Goal has been achieved within its
Time Frame based on the relevant Workout History.

[.changed]#*summarizeWeek(history: WorkoutHistory, goal: Goal, week: Week) : WeeklySummary* (Domain Concept, Category: Action)#
An action that produces an overview of Workout Sessions, Attendance
Days, Missed Days, and Goal Evaluation for a completed Week.

[.changed]#*Weekly Activity Pattern* (Domain Concept, Category: Behavior)#
A behavior composed of Workout Session has just been performed,
Workout Session has just been recorded, and Workout day has just been
missed events across the Calendar Days of a Week, together with the
resulting distribution of Attendance Days and Missed Days.

[.changed]#*Consistency* (Domain Concept, Category: Behavior)#
A behavior describing how regularly Workout Sessions occur across a Time
Frame, based on the distribution of Attendance Days and Missed Days in
the relevant Workout History and interpreted through
evaluateConsistency(...).

[.changed]#*Streak* (Domain Concept, Category: Behavior)#
A behavior composed of consecutive Attendance Days produced by repeated
Workout Session has just been performed events with no intervening
Missed Day. This behavior is extended by Streak has just been extended
and terminated by Streak has just been broken.

[.changed]#*Progress* (Domain Concept, Category: Behavior)#
A behavior composed of changes observed across Workout Sessions,
Consistency Measures, Session Comparisons, Goal Evaluations, and Weekly
Summaries over successive Time Frames.

===== Requirement-Level Concepts

[.changed]#*User* (Requirement Concept, Category: Entity)#
A person interacting with the system-to-be. In domain descriptions, the
preferred term is GymGoer. User is kept for requirements that describe
system interaction, accounts, interface behavior, or permissions.

[.changed]#*Account* (Requirement Concept, Category: Entity)#
A persistent identity that allows a User's Workout History and Goals to
be stored and retrieved by the system.

[.changed]#*Notification* (Requirement Concept, Category: Entity)#
A message delivered by the system to inform a User about reminders,
streak status, or goal progress.

[.changed]#*Weekly Summary Table* (Requirement Concept, Category: Entity)#
A structured system representation of a Weekly Summary produced for
review.

===== Implementation-Level Terms

[.changed]#*Preset Entry* (Implementation Term, Category: Entity)#
A predefined workout or activity configuration used to speed up
logging.

[.changed]#*Quick Entry Workflow* (Implementation Term, Category: Behavior)#
A sequence of interface steps designed to minimize the effort required
to record a workout.

[.changed]#*Motivational Message* (Implementation Term, Category: Entity)#
Text produced by the system to reinforce consistency or progress. It is
kept as an implementation term rather than a domain concept because the
domain-level phenomenon is motivation or encouragement, while a
motivational message is a specific system-produced textual artifact.

===== Categorization Notes

[.changed]#*Reminder* is categorized as an entity because this section refers to
the prompt or cue itself, which can exist independently of software. If
the focus were on the act of reminding, it could instead be modeled as
an action or event.#

[.changed]#*Notification* is categorized as an entity because this section refers
to the delivered message itself. If the focus were on delivery, it
could instead be modeled as an event.#

[.changed]#*Weekly Summary* is categorized as an entity, while summarizeWeek(...)
is categorized as an action. This distinguishes the produced artifact
from the domain action that produces it.#

[.changed]#*Goal Evaluation* names the action of assessing whether a Goal was
achieved. *Goal Result* names the evaluated outcome produced by that
action.#

[.changed]#*Duration* is the domain concept. Minutes may still be used as a unit
for measuring Duration, but Minutes is not modeled as the main domain
concept.#

[.changed]#*Workout Session* and *Workout History* are related through the
GymGoer. A Workout Session belongs to the Workout History of the
GymGoer who performed it.#

[.changed]#*Streak*, *Consistency*, *Progress*, and *Weekly Activity Pattern* are
categorized as behaviors because they describe temporal compositions of
events and actions rather than standalone objects.#

[.changed]#*User* is kept as a requirement-level concept for system interaction.
*GymGoer* is used as the domain role for the person performing and
reviewing fitness activity.#

===== [.changed]#Terminology Completeness Validation#

[.changed]#A terminology walkthrough was used to test whether the domain story
could be told using domain terms only.#

[.changed]#Scenario used for validation: A Goal requires three Attendance Days
within one Week. In that Week, a Workout Session has just been
performed on Monday and Tuesday. These Workout Sessions belong to the
same GymGoer’s Workout History and produce two Attendance Days. On
Wednesday, a Workout day has just been missed, so a Missed Day occurs
and the emerging Streak is broken. On Thursday, another Workout Session
has just been performed. The Workout History for that Week now contains
three Workout Sessions distributed across one Week, with three Attendance
Days and one Missed Day. From this Workout History, the domain can
compute a Streak, evaluate Consistency, evaluate the Goal within its Time
Frame, and produce a Weekly Summary.#

[.changed]#Gap discovered during walkthrough: the scenario required the terms
Workout History, Calendar Day, Time Frame, Week, Goal Evaluation,
Goal Result, Weekly Summary, Duration, Session Comparison, and the
relationship between GymGoer and Workout History. These concepts were
already used implicitly by the domain model, but they were not previously
defined in a way that made the full scenario easy to tell using only
domain terminology.#

[.changed]#Resolution: the terminology was restructured so that entities, events,
actions, and behaviors all appear together under Domain Concepts. In this
structure, Workout History contains the Workout Sessions associated with
one GymGoer; Attendance Days and Missed Days are derived from the
distribution of Workout Sessions across Calendar Days; a Week is a Time
Frame; Goal Evaluation is performed over a Time Frame; Duration replaces
Minutes as the main domain concept for time spent exercising; and
behaviors such as Streak, Consistency, Progress, and Weekly Activity
Pattern are defined as compositions of domain events and actions rather
than as isolated descriptions.#
