==== *2.1.4 Domain Narrative*

#A GymGoer performs Workout Sessions over time. A Workout Session consists of a Workout, which is a set period of time during which one or more Exercises or Activities are performed. An Exercise is a structured form of activity characterized by repetitions, duration, or intensity, while an Activity is a physical action performed for fitness purposes.#

#A Workout Session occurs on a calendar day; when a Workout Session occurs on a given day, that day is considered an Attendance Day. When no Workout Session occurs on a given day, that day is considered a Missed Day. Attendance Days and Missed Days are derived from a Workout History, which is an ordered collection of Workout Sessions across time. As Workout Sessions occur or are skipped, the distribution of Attendance Days and Missed Days evolves over time, forming patterns of activity. From this distribution, patterns such as Consistency and Streaks can be determined. Consistency represents the regularity with which Workout Sessions occur over time, while a Streak is defined as a sequence of consecutive Attendance Days. When a Workout Session occurs on the day immediately following an Attendance Day, the Streak is extended; when a Missed Day follows one or more Attendance Days, the Streak is broken.#

#Workout Sessions are further evaluated within a Time Frame, where activity is aggregated and compared against defined Goals. At the completion of a Time Frame, the sequence of Workout Sessions within the Workout History is used to evaluate whether a Goal has been achieved. These evaluations rely on the distribution of Attendance Days and Missed Days within the Time Frame and contribute to the overall assessment of Consistency and Progress.#

A gym routine in the domain is made up of Workout Sessions distributed across time. #A Workout Session may consist of strength training, cardio, or a combination of both, and is characterized by observable attributes such as duration, type of training, and the Exercises performed. These attributes describe the structure of each session and allow Workout Sessions to be distinguished from one another. The composition and ordering of Workout Sessions within a Workout History allow deviation between sessions to be observed over time, including variations in Exercises, duration, and training type.#
