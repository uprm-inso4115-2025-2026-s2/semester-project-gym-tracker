==== *2.1.4 Domain Narrative*

#A GymGoer performs Workout Sessions over time. A Workout Session is a specific occurrence of a Workout performed by a GymGoer on a particular Calendar Day. A Workout is a bounded period of physical training during which one or more Exercises or Activities are performed. An Exercise is a structured unit of activity characterized by repetitions, duration, or intensity, while an Activity is a physical action performed for fitness purposes.#

#Workout Sessions are accumulated in a Workout History, which is an ordered collection of Workout Sessions associated with a GymGoer across time. From this Workout History, the domain can retrieveWorkoutHistory(...), evaluateConsistency(...), computeStreak(...), evaluateGoal(...), and summarizeWeek(...).#

#A Workout Session occurs on a Calendar Day; when at least one Workout Session occurs on a given Calendar Day, that day is considered an Attendance Day. When no Workout Session occurs on a given Calendar Day despite an intended or expected workout pattern, that day is considered a Missed Day. Attendance Days and Missed Days are therefore derived from the distribution of Workout Sessions across Calendar Days within a Workout History.#

#As Workout Sessions are performed or skipped, the Workout History gives rise to domain behaviors. Consistency is the behavior formed by repeated Workout Session has just been performed and Workout Session has just been skipped events across a Time Frame. A Streak is the behavior formed by consecutive Attendance Days with no intervening Missed Day. A Streak is extended when a Workout Session has just been performed on the Calendar Day immediately following an Attendance Day, and it is broken when a Missed Day occurs after one or more Attendance Days. Progress is observed through changes across Workout Sessions, Session Comparisons, Consistency Measures, and Goal Evaluations over successive Time Frames.#

#Workout Sessions are further evaluated within a Time Frame, such as a Week, where activity is aggregated and compared against defined Goals. A Goal specifies a desired level of activity within a Time Frame. At the completion of a Time Frame, the relevant portion of the Workout History can be used to evaluateGoal(...) and produce a Weekly Summary through summarizeWeek(...). These evaluations rely on the distribution of Attendance Days and Missed Days within the Time Frame and contribute to the assessment of Consistency and Progress.#

#A Workout Session may consist of strength training, cardio, or a combination of both, and may be described by observable attributes such as duration, training type, Exercises performed, and Workout Notes. These attributes allow Workout Sessions to be compared through compareSessions(...) and distinguished from one another across the Workout History.#
