==== 2.2.3 Domain Requirements

*Session Representation*
#The system must provide means to represent each Workout Session as a distinct entity associated with:#
#• Specific Calendar Day#
#• Session type#
#• Duration#
#• Exercises performed#

*Scheduling*
#The system must provide means to associate each Workout Session with a specific Calendar Day to represent the distribution of Workout Sessions across time.#

*Goal Representation*
#The system must provide means to define a Goal associated with a specific Time Frame.#

#Inconsistency case RT-2#
#Inconsistency: Earlier discussion referred to both daily goals and weekly
goals, but the requirements in this section were being written around weekly
evaluation. That left the scope of Goal unclear.#

#Method used: Resolution tactic - scope narrowing.#

#Candidate resolution: In the current milestone, Goal is treated in the core
domain as a weekly goal tied to a Time Frame. Daily goals may be added later,
but they are not part of the core requirement set now.#

*Goal Evaluation*
The system must provide means to evaluate whether recorded Workout Sessions satisfy a defined Goal within its Time Frame.

#Inconsistency case FE-2#
#Inconsistency: It was unclear whether weekly goal evaluation should count
Workout Sessions or active days, since more than one session can happen on the
same day.#

#Method used: Further elicitation through a stakeholder walkthrough based on
the question: if someone works out twice on Tuesday, does that count as one day
toward the weekly goal or as two completed workouts?#

#Candidate resolution: In the core domain, weekly goals are evaluated by active
days within the Time Frame. Multiple Workout Sessions on the same day remain in
the history, but they count as one Attendance Day for weekly goal evaluation.#

*Low-Effort Logging*
The system must provide means to record a Workout Session through a Quick Entry Workflow that minimizes required input steps.

#Inconsistency case RT-3#
#Inconsistency: The documentation asks for quick logging with little effort,
but it also expects enough detail for later comparison and reflection. Those
two aims pull in different directions.#

#Method used: Resolution tactic - default plus optional extension.#

#Candidate resolution: The base logging flow requires only the information
needed to register a Workout Session. Exercises and notes remain optional.
This keeps logging light while still allowing more detail when a person wants
to add it.#

*Persistent Storage and Retrieval*
#The system must provide means to store Workout Sessions persistently and retrieve them in chronological order as Workout History.#

#Inconsistency case FE-3#
#Inconsistency: The documentation allowed old Workout Sessions to be edited,
but it did not make clear whether a correction to past data should change
derived results such as summaries and streak-related values.#

#Method used: Further elicitation through a correction scenario walkthrough:
a person forgets to log Friday's workout and adds it later, or fixes the date
of an older session. Should the summary and streak remain as they were, or
change?#

#Candidate resolution: Stored Workout Sessions remain the source of truth. When
a past Workout Session is added or corrected, derived results such as Weekly
Summaries and Streak-related values are recalculated from the updated Workout
History.#

*Consistency Representation*
#The system must provide means to compute and represent a Consistency Measure based on the distribution of Workout Sessions across a Time Frame.#

#Inconsistency case RT-1#
#Inconsistency: Consistency and Streak were being used too closely in earlier
discussion, even though they do not mean the same thing.#

#Method used: Resolution tactic - term separation.#

#Candidate resolution: Consistency refers to regular workout behavior across
time more broadly. Streak refers only to consecutive Attendance Days. The two
ideas are related, but they should not be used as substitutes in the
requirements.#

*Streak Representation*
#The system must provide means to determine and represent a Streak as a sequence of consecutive Attendance Days derived from the Workout History.#

*Streak Calculation*
#The system must provide means to compute whether the distribution of Workout Sessions across Calendar Days extends or breaks an existing Streak based on consecutive Attendance Days and intervening Missed Days.#

#Inconsistency case FE-1#
#Inconsistency: It was unclear whether a Streak should depend on the day a
Workout Session was performed or the day it was logged into the system.#

#Method used: Further elicitation through stakeholder questioning based on the
scenario: a person works out on Monday night but records it on Tuesday morning.
Which day should count toward the streak?#

#Candidate resolution: Streak calculation is based on the Calendar Day on which
the Workout Session was performed, not the time of data entry. Logging records
the session, but the Streak reflects the real attendance pattern.#

*Weekly Aggregation*
#The system must provide means to aggregate Workout Sessions within a Week as a Time Frame.#

*Weekly Comparison*
#The system must provide means to compare Workout Sessions, Attendance Days, or related summary results in the current Week with those of previous Weeks.#

*Weekly Summary Representation*
#The system must provide means to generate a Weekly Summary that aggregates Workout Sessions, Attendance Days, Missed Days, and Goal evaluation within a Week.#

*Essential Information Recording*
#The system must provide means to record a Workout Session using only essential information: session type, duration, and optional short notes.#

*Separation of Nutrition Tracking*
#The system must provide means to record a Workout Session without requiring Nutrition information as a precondition.#

#*Workout Ownership*#
#The system must provide means to associate Workout Sessions with a specific GymGoer, ensuring that each GymGoer's Workout History is distinct and private.#

#*Data Validation Rules*#
#The system must provide means to enforce data validation rules for Workout Session entries, such as preventing future Calendar Days and ensuring required attributes are provided.#
